# Homepage visual direction — 7 October 2026

## Usaha kecil, panggung besar

The homepage sells content and advertising services to Indonesian culinary and hospitality businesses. Artwork should make those businesses feel expressive, credible, and worth noticing. This direction follows the existing white, charcoal, and chartreuse brand while introducing editorial talent photography, deliberate product perspective, and sculptural CGI.

User requirements: composed creative key visuals rather than generic photographs, consistency across media and sections, artwork suited to the existing website. The user authorized generation and replacement of homepage visuals.

Creative assumptions: keep the existing homepage copy and service structure; use illustrative fictional talent and businesses. Main website headings, package prices, and interactive CTAs remain editable website text. Generated artwork does not demonstrate client results.

## Shared treatment

- Brand palette: chartreuse `#dfd500`, white `#ffffff`, charcoal `#191919`; supporting ivory, warm wood, espresso and natural skin tones.
- Light: generous soft key light from upper left, warm neutral highlights, clean dimensional contact shadows.
- Materials: tactile photography, matte chartreuse sculptural geometry, charcoal device frames, cream architectural staging.
- Composition: one clear focal relationship, directional gesture or flowing curved geometry; varied viewpoints and media across sections.
- Talent: fictional Indonesian adults, natural skin and expressions, ivory/chartreuse wardrobe accents. No implication of real endorsements.
- Website fit: portrait 4:5 artwork for existing hero, service cards and AI section. Subjects and important objects remain within a generous central safe area for narrow screens. Separate mobile artwork is needed only if actual layout inspection reveals destructive cropping.
- Typography: no baked website headline or CTA. The food campaign may include the intrinsic poster headline `MENU ANDALAN`; inspect its spelling and hierarchy before use.
- Exclusions: floating screenshots as decoration, fake results/metrics, decorative sparkles, inconsistent yellow/orange palette shifts, unrelated brand logos.
